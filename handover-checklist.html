Use this example to agree evidence for a scoped Wazuh engagement. No item below represents a completed client test.

SAMPLE TEMPLATE — NOT A CLIENT REPORT

Scope & ownership

  • Client and project owner identified
  • Asset and log source scope agreed
  • Wazuh and integration versions recorded
  • Required access, dependencies and exclusions agreed

Evidence / owner / result: to be recorded during the engagement

Monitoring validation

  • Expected source events collected
  • Required fields decoded correctly
  • Positive and negative detection tests recorded
  • Alerts indexed and visible in dashboards
  • Notifications reach agreed destinations

Evidence / owner / result: to be recorded during the engagement

Operations & resilience

  • Agent and integration health checks defined
  • Retention and storage assumptions recorded
  • Backup and recovery procedure agreed and tested where scoped
  • Rollback plan and change record documented

Evidence / owner / result: to be recorded during the engagement

Handover

  • Configuration inventory supplied
  • Runbooks and known limitations supplied
  • Training and acceptance review completed
  • Access removal or ongoing access agreed
  • Support scope and escalation contacts recorded

Evidence / owner / result: to be recorded during the engagement
